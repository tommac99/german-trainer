# Deutsch üben

A free German practice app for learners from A1 to B2.

- **Wortschatz (vocabulary):** about 2,900 words from A1 to B2 with article, plural and English. Rounds use spaced repetition: words you get wrong come back first, words you know come back after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 days, and every round drips in a few new words from the level you choose.
- **Grammatik (grammar):** 25 topics and 415 questions on verb position, haben/sein, Partizip II, Präsens, Präteritum, Futur, Modalverben, Konjunktiv II, Passiv, the four cases, articles, prepositions, Wechselpräpositionen, adjective endings and relative pronouns. The newest block, "Latest lesson", follows Sophie's Dativ oder Akkusativ class: the indirect object, verbs with fixed prepositions, dem/den and einem/einen, adjective endings and reflexive verbs. Verb topics also mix in fresh drills built from the word list.
- **Help while you practise:** every German sentence has its English in brackets, a "Show rules or tips" button before you answer, a lesson after each mistake, and full conjugation tables (ich, du, er, wir, ihr, sie in Präsens, Präteritum and Perfekt) for the verbs in the question.
- **Report for your teacher:** the Progress tab shows results by topic, vocabulary learnt per level, words you struggle with and every mistake. Copy it as text, download a CSV or print it.

No sign-up, no server. Answers are saved only in your own browser.

Keys: 1 to 4 pick an answer, H shows tips, Enter goes to the next question.

The word lists are our own, graded by CEFR level. They are not the official Goethe-Institut lists.
