# Deutsch üben

A free German practice app for B2 learners. 20 topics, 310 multiple-choice questions, every German sentence with its English in brackets, a "Show rules or tips" button before each answer, a lesson after each mistake, and a progress log you can export (text, CSV or PDF) to send to a teacher.

No sign-up, no server. Answers are saved only in your own browser.

Topics: verb position, haben/sein, Partizip II, Präsens, Präteritum, Futur, Modalverben, Konjunktiv II, Passiv, the four cases, articles, prepositions, Wechselpräpositionen, adjective endings, relative pronouns.

Keys: 1 to 4 pick an answer, H shows tips, Enter goes to the next question.
